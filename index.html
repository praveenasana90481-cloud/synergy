School Assembly Presentation

CLASS 10 F - SYNERGY

Together We Are Stronger

Class 10 F presented an inspiring assembly on Habit 6: Synergize. Through explanation, teamwork activities, a skit and performance, students showed that respecting differences and combining individual strengths can create extraordinary results.

1

Learning Through a Skit

The “Board Exam Project” skit demonstrated how a group initially struggles when members work separately. Once they listen to one another, share responsibilities and recognise each person’s talent, the group becomes more confident and successful.

Message: Teamwork turns individual effort into shared success.
2

The 4 C’s of Teamwork

Students explained that effective synergy depends on four important qualities: Clarity in goals, Connection among members, Commitment to the task and meaningful Contribution from everyone. These qualities help a team work with trust and purpose.

Clarity • Connection • Commitment • Contribution
3

One Class, One Purpose

The programme concluded with a joyful group moment that reflected the true spirit of synergy. Every student played a role, supported others and helped create a memorable assembly. Class 10 F proved that unity does not remove differences—it uses them as strengths.

Result: Different talents came together as one united team.
